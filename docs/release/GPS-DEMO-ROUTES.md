# GPS demo routes

The links below use the local production preview on port 4180. On a phone or tablet, use the team's HTTPS build and copy the part of the link starting at `#/explore`. GPS offsets in this guide are measured from the first accepted Earth fix. The map coordinates are Mars planetocentric latitude and east longitude. Use the share links for full coordinate precision. Choose **Shortest** when the app offers multiple paths.

## 1. Replan through an unvisited stop

[Open the two-stop Wildcat Ridge round trip](http://127.0.0.1:4180/#/explore?mode=2d&tab=marswalk&plan=%7B%22version%22%3A2%2C%22data%22%3A%22mola-jezero-gale-4%22%2C%22start%22%3A%7B%22lat%22%3A18.455690972861863%2C%22lon%22%3A77.40794456514794%2C%22name%22%3A%22Test%20lander%22%7D%2C%22blocked%22%3A%7B%22lat%22%3A18.45761422181569%2C%22lon%22%3A77.4069154582516%7D%2C%22stops%22%3A%5B%7B%22point%22%3A%7B%22lat%22%3A18.456669467943634%2C%22lon%22%3A77.40701668188075%2C%22name%22%3A%22Science%20stop%20A%22%7D%2C%22scienceMinutes%22%3A3%7D%2C%7B%22point%22%3A%7B%22lat%22%3A18.45864728%2C%22lon%22%3A77.40592495%2C%22name%22%3A%22Science%20stop%20B%22%7D%2C%22scienceMinutes%22%3A7%7D%5D%2C%22returnToLander%22%3Atrue%2C%22slope%22%3A25%2C%22weighted%22%3Afalse%2C%22mission%22%3A%7B%22speed%22%3A0.7%2C%22stop%22%3A30%2C%22budget%22%3A180%2C%22reserve%22%3A30%2C%22departure%22%3A%222026-11-15T07%3A00%22%2C%22rover%22%3Afalse%2C%22roverSpeed%22%3A1.2%2C%22roverSlope%22%3A10%7D%7D)

- Lander · 18.455691° N, 77.407945° E
- Science stop A · 18.456669° N, 77.407017° E · 52.2 m west and 58.0 m north of the first Earth fix at 1×.
- Science stop B · 18.458647° N, 77.405925° E
- Simulated blocked cell · 18.457614° N, 77.406915° E · already in the link.
- Off-route test point · 18.456298° N, 77.407017° E · 52.2 m west and 36.0 m north of the first Earth fix at 1×.

On a phone, choose **Start route → Earth GPS Demo**, select 1×, and allow precise location. Start in a large open area. Reach A, confirm that B becomes the next stop, and move roughly 22 m south from A. Stay there for several successive fixes. The test replayed precise fixes over about five seconds.

The replay showed **Route updated through your remaining stops**. The new route still visits B and returns to the lander. The calculated remainder was 384.7 m with the replan and 386.2 m if it rejoined the previous route. That difference was within the replayed GPS uncertainty, so the shortcut announcement was withheld. When the replay added another 900 m east, the app reported that the planned route was about 900 m west and gave a west arrow. Returning to the off-route test point resumed guidance.

If you're walking a physical test, stay within an accessible open area and use the 22 m local deviation. Use injected fixes for the 900 m case.

**Shorter route alert.** Restart the same plan and reach A. Try the alternate off-route point at 18.455995° N, 77.406342° E, about 90.1 m west and 18.0 m north of the first Earth fix at 1×. With 2 m accuracy in the replay, the app found **387.6 m** through B and back to the lander versus **421.4 m** via the old route. It showed **Shorter route found**, which has a bundled voice clip.
