# Drone View: verified areas and candidate datasets

## Available in this branch

- **Wildcat Ridge:** aligned Mars 2020 HiRISE orthoimage and 1 m elevation crop. This patch is 384 m square. [USGS Jezero landing site products](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars-2020-jezero-crater-landing-site-products).
- **Gale Crater / Mount Sharp:** NASA Mars Trek Curiosity HiRISE mosaic and measured 20 m evaluation grid. The exported crop bounds and processing steps are in [Gale terrain sources](GALE-TERRAIN.md). The app only opens flight where the source image and terrain overlap.
- **Global view:** Viking color imagery covers all Mars in 2D and 3D. The `Frontend` 3840 × 1920 image matches the existing 2048 × 1024 mosaic without a longitude shift (normalized luminance correlation 0.9883 after resampling).
- **Online flyovers and close zoom:** NASA Mars Trek streams numerical HRSC/MOLA elevation at about 200 m and Viking color imagery at about 232 m across nearly all Mars. The simulated camera stays higher on this data. A point needs valid imagery and elevation; otherwise the globe retains its wider view. The app also requests finer Mars Trek terrain in Gale, Columbia Hills, Victoria Crater, Endeavour Crater and the InSight region. Those sources have patchy footprints. Check the displayed source at the selected location. [NASA Mars Trek API](https://trek.nasa.gov/tiles/apidoc/trekAPI.html?body=mars)
- **USGS archive search:** Drone View searches HiRISE and CTX products at the selected point. It reads a small window from the source GeoTIFF, checks elevation and NoData, then uses a matching orbital image when available. Olympus Mons south flank was verified with HiRISE elevation and Viking imagery. Discover shows shortcuts and saved flyovers. **Save area offline** stores the loaded elevation and image window in that browser. Install **Planet map and app** in the production build to reopen it without a connection. [USGS HiRISE](https://stac.astrogeology.usgs.gov/docs/data/mars/hirise_dtms/) · [USGS CTX](https://stac.astrogeology.usgs.gov/docs/data/mars/ctxdtms/)
- **Bundled flight fallback:** If the online elevation or image fails to load, Drone View uses the bundled 0.25° MOLA elevation and Viking imagery. This is a higher-altitude view with about 14.8 km equatorial cell spacing. The production offline pack includes these assets.
- **Itinerary routing:** Each directed leg uses Wildcat Ridge HiRISE (1 m), Gale (20 m), or bundled MOLA (0.25°), in that order when both endpoints are inside the same applicable grid. Each leg reports its source and sampling. A failed leg leaves other legs visible. MOLA can model regional routes and cannot resolve walking-scale conditions.

## Candidates for coverage expansion

1. **Western Jezero and the Perseverance traverse:** The [USGS Mars Sample Return release](https://www.usgs.gov/data/mars-sample-return-hirise-mosaics-terrain-relative-navigation) includes a DTM, a paired orthoimage and a confidence mosaic from eight HiRISE stereo pairs. Use the orthorectified imagery as the texture, the DTM as the mesh and confidence plus NoData to decide where flight and routing are available. Treat this as a separate terrain pack; the producer reports meaningful offsets between the new and original Jezero mosaics. Inspect overlaps before bridging routes between them. [Producer alignment report](https://www.hou.usra.edu/meetings/lpsc2025/pdf/1627.pdf).
2. **Spirit / Columbia Hills:** The online flyover uses Mars Trek elevation with Viking color. The [HiRISE DTM archive](https://www.uahirise.org/hiwish/maps/dtms.jsp) includes stereo product DTEEC_001513_1655_001777_1650_U01. Verify alignment with the associated orthoimage and mission pins before bundling high-resolution imagery.
3. **InSight:** The online flyover uses the Mars Trek InSight DEM mosaic and CTX imagery. USGS publishes [CTX elevation](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/ctx_digital_terrain_model_of_mars_insight_landing_site_ellipse_9_b_west) and [a CTX orthoimage](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/ctx_orthoimage_of_mars_insight_landing_site) as candidates for an offline pack.
4. **Other locations:** Browse the [USGS HiRISE DTM and orthoimage catalog](https://stac.astrogeology.usgs.gov/docs/data/mars/hirise_dtms/) for smaller detailed sites, or the [USGS CTX DTM and orthoimage archive](https://stac.astrogeology.usgs.gov/docs/data/mars/ctxdtms/) for wider 20 m regional patches. A rover photograph or an oblique orbital image alone cannot provide a navigable terrain mesh.

For each additional offline pack, keep the original product IDs, verify projection and vertical datum, export aligned crops, retain validity masks, and test a known point plus the coverage boundary.
